# RFC 0023: Federation Jurisdictional Defaults

- Author(s): TBD
- Status: open (v1.1 open question)
- Type: standards-track
- Created: 2026-05-23
- Targets: v1.1

## Summary

A proposed table of jurisdiction-pair defaults for federation (e.g., EEA cross-border under SCCs; US-EU under DPF; UK-EEA under UK GDPR adequacy).

## Motivation

Operators currently set federation jurisdiction policy manually. A community-maintained baseline table reduces operator burden and surfaces policy gaps.

## Open questions

- Who maintains the table (steward, working group, or external authority)?
- How to handle policy changes mid-federation (a previously adequate jurisdiction loses adequacy)?
- Whether to encode jurisdiction policy in the trust anchor or in a separate signed object.

Unresolved at v1.0-rc1.
