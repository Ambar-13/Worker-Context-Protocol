## Seat composition guidance (per TSC_BYLAWS.md)

- At minimum 5 seated members at the time of acceptance.
- Seats SHOULD represent at least three distinct affiliations to satisfy the cross-vendor neutrality goal.
- At least one seat MUST be designated as an Independent (no current employment relationship with any operator deploying WCP), per TSC_BYLAWS.md Section 3.4.
- Chair is elected by simple majority of seated members at the first meeting.
