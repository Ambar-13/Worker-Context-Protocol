# WCP Trademark Policy

## Marks covered

This policy governs use of the following marks:

- "WCP"
- "Worker Context Protocol"
- "WCP-conformant"
- "WCP Level N" (where N is 1, 2, or 3, per `spec/conformance.md`)
- Any logos published under `branding/` in this repository

## Status

| Period | Mark holder | Enforcement |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-v1.0 final | Rentably Pte Ltd (UEN 202612506Z) | **Non-enforcement commitment** (this document) |
| Post-v1.0 final | Steward (per `DONATION_COMMITMENT.md`) | Steward's published policy |

## Non-enforcement commitment (pre-v1.0 final)

Rentably commits, in writing, to not enforce these marks against any party for the duration of the pre-v1.0 final period. This includes:

- Implementers describing their software as "WCP-compatible" or "implementing WCP".
- Operators describing their marketplaces as "running WCP".
- Documentation, papers, and talks referencing WCP by name.

Rentably reserves the right to **publicly identify** incompatible forks or claims that present themselves as conformant without passing the conformance suite. This is a clarification mechanism, not a legal action.

## Acceptable use of "WCP-conformant"

A party MAY claim "WCP-conformant at Level N" only if:

1. They hold a passing conformance report against the current suite version (or a version within the deprecation overlap window per `spec/semver-policy.md`).
2. The report was generated by the official conformance suite from `conformance/`.
3. They are prepared to share the report URL on request.

A party MAY claim "WCP-compatible" without a conformance report; this is a weaker claim and does not invoke the conformance suite. "WCP-compatible" implies the party speaks the protocol; "WCP-conformant at Level N" implies they have passed the test bundle at that level.

## Logo use

If logos are published under `branding/` (none exist at v0.2):

- The unmodified logo MAY be used to refer to WCP in documentation, talks, and marketing material referring to the protocol.
- Derivative logos (e.g., "WCP-Compatible Robot Vendor" badges) MAY be created provided they are visually distinct from the canonical mark and do not imply unilateral endorsement by Rentably or the steward.
- The canonical mark MUST NOT be used as part of a product name, company name, or service name in a way that implies the WCP project, Rentably, or the steward is the source of that product.

## Domain names

`wcp-spec.org` (target; not yet registered at v0.2) is reserved for the project. Other domain names containing "WCP" are not restricted; choose names that do not imply endorsement.

## Reporting misuse

Pre-v1.0 final, report potential misuse to `governance@rentably.ai`. We will engage in good faith; legal escalation is a last resort and not the default response.

## Post-donation transition

When the donation in `DONATION_COMMITMENT.md` is effected, the marks transfer to the steward. The steward's published policy will supersede this document; Rentably commits to a 30-day overlap during which both this policy and the steward's policy apply.

## Statement of purpose

The intent of this policy is to keep "WCP-conformant" a meaningful claim (it requires passing the suite) while making "WCP" itself freely usable in description, documentation, and ecosystem-building. The protocol's value depends on adoption, and adoption depends on parties feeling free to reference and build on the protocol without legal friction.
