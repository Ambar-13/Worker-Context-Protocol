# Worker Context Protocol: Governance

WCP is published by Rentably Pte Ltd (UEN 202612506Z) under the Apache License 2.0. This document is the overview and pointer; binding commitments live in the documents named below.

## Documents

- **[DONATION_COMMITMENT.md](./DONATION_COMMITMENT.md)** binds Rentably to donate WCP stewardship to a neutral foundation by v1.0.
- **[NON_COERCION_COMMITMENT.md](./NON_COERCION_COMMITMENT.md)** binds Rentably's marketplace to a 5x integration-time ratio between WCP and non-WCP integrations.
- **[CHARTER.md](./CHARTER.md)** defines the Technical Steering Committee (TSC) composition target, worker-class representation balance, and decision rights.
- **[RFC_PROCESS.md](./RFC_PROCESS.md)** defines how the spec evolves through RFCs with lazy consensus and TSC review.

## Roles at v0.1

| Role | Holder | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Interim chair | Rentably (principal) | until first non-Rentably TSC member seated (target Month 2) |
| Reference implementations | Rentably (ROS 2 plugin, PWA module, FastAPI backend) | Apache 2.0 |
| Trademark holder ("WCP-conformant") | Rentably -> steward at v1.0 | non-enforcement commitment pre-v1.0 |

## Trajectory

Month 0 (v0.1): published with this governance commitment.
Month 2: first non-Rentably TSC members seated.
Month 4 (v0.5): TSC at 5 members with balanced worker-class representation.
Month 6: formal application to a steward (Linux Foundation, IEEE RAS, ROS-Industrial, OASIS Open).
Month 12 (v1.0): donation effected; TSC at 7 members including a steward representative.
