# WCP Non-Coercion Commitment

Rentably's marketplace (rentably.ai) remains open to non-WCP integrations.

## The 5x ratio

The integration-time ratio between WCP and non-WCP integrations into Rentably's marketplace shall not exceed 5x. Specifically:

| Integration type | WCP target | Non-WCP commitment |
|---|---|---|
| Robot vendor | under 8 hours | within 40 hours |
| Human-worker platform | under 2 hours | within 10 hours |

## What is bound

Rentably will not condition any of the following on the use of WCP:

- Marketplace access (any verified worker provider may list, WCP or not)
- Payment terms (settlement schedules apply equally)
- Matching priority (the matching engine treats WCP and non-WCP capability descriptors equivalently)
- Customer-side promotion

## How it is measured

Onboarding times are timestamped from the first integration ticket open to the first successful production job. Quarterly reports of the WCP-to-non-WCP ratio are posted publicly at https://wcp-spec.rentably.ai/non-coercion-report.

## Breach

Breach of the 5x ratio is grounds for the Technical Steering Committee (per [CHARTER.md](./CHARTER.md)) to revoke Rentably's status as a reference implementation. Revocation requires TSC simple-majority quorum and 14 days' notice.

## Scope

This commitment applies to Rentably's marketplace operations. It does not bind other WCP-implementing coordinators; they SHOULD adopt similar non-coercion commitments and are encouraged to publish them.
