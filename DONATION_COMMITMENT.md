# WCP Donation Commitment

The Worker Context Protocol is published by Rentably Pte Ltd (UEN 202612506Z) under the Apache License 2.0.

Rentably commits, in writing, to donate stewardship of WCP to a neutral open-source foundation no later than the v1.0 release.

## Candidates under active evaluation

- Linux Foundation (LF AI & Data project model)
- IEEE Robotics and Automation Society (P-projects)
- ROS-Industrial Consortium
- OASIS Open

Final steward selection rests with the Technical Steering Committee defined in [CHARTER.md](./CHARTER.md).

## Scope of donation

The donation includes:

1. The `wcp-spec` repository and its history.
2. The `did:wcp` method registration and any namespaces.
3. The "WCP-conformant" trademark.
4. The conformance test suite.
5. The RFC process and its archives.

Rentably retains no exclusive control over the WCP specification, the WCP trademark, or the conformance process beyond what any other member of the chosen steward holds.

## Binding clause

This commitment is binding on Rentably Pte Ltd, its successors, and its assigns. Any acquisition of Rentably Pte Ltd does not transfer exclusive control of WCP to the acquirer. In the event of acquisition before v1.0, the donation MUST be completed by the acquirer within 90 days of the transaction closing, or the TSC MAY effect the donation independently with reference implementations forked as necessary.

## Pre-v1.0 trademark non-enforcement

Until v1.0 and the donation is effected, Rentably commits to not enforce the "WCP-conformant" trademark against any implementation. Rentably reserves the right to publicly call out incompatible forks that present themselves as conformant.
