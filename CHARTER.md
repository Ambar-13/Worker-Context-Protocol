# Worker Context Protocol: Technical Steering Committee Charter

## TSC composition target by month

| Month | Min Members | Worker-Class Representation | New Slot Added | Org-Cap Check (max from one org) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 (v0.1 publication) | 1 | seed: principal serves as interim chair | principal as interim chair | 1 of 1 OK |
| 2 | 3 | at least 1 academic, 1 worker provider (any class) | first two non-Rentably TSC members seated | 2 of 3 OK |
| 4 (v0.5) | 5 | at least 1 academic, 1 robot-side provider, 1 human-side provider, 1 independent | balanced worker-class coverage achieved | 2 of 5 |
| 12 (v1.0) | 7 | as above plus 1 steward representative | steward seat occupied | 2 of 7 |

Worker-class balance is the discipline that prevents WCP from drifting back to robot-only or human-only framing in TSC decisions. A TSC majority that lacks worker-class balance is grounds for the wider community to file a no-confidence RFC.

## Decision rights

- **Lazy consensus** on `rfcs/*` PRs after 14 days open with no substantive objection from a TSC member or 3 or more community members.
- **TSC simple-majority quorum** required for breaking changes to `spec/*`.
- **TSC unanimous** required for amendments to this charter or to [DONATION_COMMITMENT.md](./DONATION_COMMITMENT.md) or [NON_COERCION_COMMITMENT.md](./NON_COERCION_COMMITMENT.md) pre-donation.
- **Emergency flag** bypasses the 14-day window with explicit TSC sign-off and a post-hoc public note within 7 days.

## Eligibility

A TSC seat may be held by:

- A named individual (not a corporate seat) representing an organization with active WCP engagement (committed RFCs, shipping implementation, deployed pilots, or recognized research).
- A named individual representing themselves as an independent (not affiliated with any party that has WCP commercial interest).

The chair role rotates annually. The interim chair (Rentably principal at v0.1) steps down at Month 2 once non-Rentably TSC members are seated.

## Term and rotation

TSC seats are 2-year renewable terms. At least one seat opens every 12 months to prevent stasis. Renewal requires majority TSC vote.

## Vacancy

A vacant seat is filled within 60 days. Nominations come from the community; the TSC selects from the nominee pool with simple majority. If the TSC cannot fill a seat within 60 days, the wider community may convene a 30-day open nomination and vote process.

## Conflict of interest

TSC members MUST recuse themselves from decisions where they or their employer have direct commercial interest beyond ordinary WCP implementation. Recusal is recorded in the decision log.

## Post-donation

When the donation in [DONATION_COMMITMENT.md](./DONATION_COMMITMENT.md) is effected, this charter is superseded by the steward's governance documents. The TSC continues as a project-level body within the steward's structure.
